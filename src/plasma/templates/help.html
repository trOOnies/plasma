Welcome to plasma!

🧭 Getting around

🏠 Home

Click plasma for your stats, what's fresh, what's coming, and where you left off.

📺 Series, 🎞️ Movies & 🎮 Games

Browse Series, Movies or Games, then search or filter by status and favorites. Long lists split into pages, with buttons at the top and bottom.

📡 Platforms

Your streaming services and game stores live in one central list.

➕ New stuff

Use + Series, + Movie or + Game at the top right to add something new.

✅ Tracking what you watch

👆 One click, all caught up

Click an episode number and everything before it gets marked too. Mark season watched does the same for earlier seasons.

⏪ Oops, went too far?

Click an earlier watched episode to jump back to it. Everything after it gets unmarked, including the watch dates, so click carefully!

↩️ Just one step back

Click your last watched episode to unmark only that one. Clear season unmarks a single season.

⏭️ Next up

The Watched S01E02 button marks your next episode, right from the title page or home.

🔎 Season & episode pages

Click a season's name to see all its episodes, then click an episode for its own page. Each one has an Edit button for durations, release dates and descriptions.

🗓️ Upcoming & Just released

Home shows releases from the last 30 days and the next 90. New season coming? Give its first episode a release date. ✨

🎮 Tracking your games

Games work just like movies, but with a controller in your hands. Open a game and click Planned, Playing, Completed or Dropped. 🕹️

  • ⏱️ Keep count of your hours played under Edit. We won't judge.
  • 🏠 Games you're playing show up under Playing now on the home page.
  • 🏆 Mark one Completed and today becomes its finish date, unless you already set one.
  • 📦 Click Mark as owned on a game you've got, then tick Owned on the Games tab to see your collection.

🗂️ Plans

Plans are playlists for real life! Mix series, movies and games any way you like: family movie night 👨‍👩‍👧, game night with friends 🎲, couch time with your S.O. 🛋️, or a solo weekend 🧘.

  • ➕ Make one on the Plans tab, then add titles from its page.
  • 👆 Or click a plan's name on any title's page to add or remove it in one go.
  • 💖 Favorites is built in and always there. It holds everything with a filled heart, so click a heart to add or remove something.

🚦 How status works

New titles start with no status at all. Planned is your call! Click a status on a title's page to set it, and click it again to clear it.

  • 📌 Planned puts it on your Up next list.
  • ▶️ Watching turns on by itself once you mark a series' first episode.
  • 🏁 Completed marks every episode of a series watched.
  • 🗑️ Dropped sticks until you pick something else. No hard feelings.

💡 Greyed-out buttons on a series don't match your episodes. Add a new season to a finished show and it hops back to Watching.

💖 Favorites, descriptions & notes

  • 🩷 Click the heart next to a title's name to make it a favorite.
  • 🏢 Add a producer to any series, movie or game under Edit.
  • 📝 The description says what it's about, and notes are all yours. Both are under Edit.
  • 👀 Hover over an episode number for a quick peek at its details.

📡 Where to watch

Add your services and stores on the Platforms tab, then tick them when you edit a title. Each platform has a page listing everything you've got on it. 🎉

📥 Adding lots of titles at once

List your titles in info/library.yaml and run make import.

📄 Show me an example 
movies:
  - name: Arrival
    year: 2016
    status: completed
    rating: 9
    duration: 116
    release_date: 2016-11-11
    platforms: [Netflix]
    description: A linguist is asked to talk to the aliens.
    notes: Worth a rewatch.
    image: arrival.jpg

series:
  - name: The Wire
    year: 2002
    seasons: [13, 12, 12, 13, 10]
    watched_through: S02E04
    episodes:
      S01E01:
        duration: 60
        release_date: 2002-06-02
        description: The first episode.

games:
  - name: Hades
    year: 2020
    producer: Supergiant Games
    status: playing
    hours_played: 42
    owned: true
    platforms: [Steam]

👍 Importing again is safe. It won't undo anything you changed here, and only fills in what's still blank.

🖼️ Adding images

  1. Drop a JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or AVIF into info/images/.
  2. Add its file name to the title, such as image: arrival.jpg.
  3. Run make import. Ta-da! ✨

🚪 Leaving plasma

The door button at the top right stops plasma and closes the page. Your changes save as you go, and anything typed into an open form is saved first.

🖐️ Browsers only let pages close themselves sometimes. If the tab stays open, plasma has still stopped, so just close it yourself. Run make serve to come back!
