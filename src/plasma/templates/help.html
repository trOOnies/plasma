Welcome to plasma!

Your own little corner for every series and movie you love, hate-watch, or swear you'll start next week. 😄

🔒 Everything stays right here on your computer.

🧭 Getting around

🏠 Home

Click plasma for your stats, what's fresh, what's coming, and where you left off.

📺 Series, 🎞️ Movies & 🎮 Games

Browse Series, Movies or Games, each shown as a grid of images, then search or filter by status, genre, age restriction and favorites. Long lists split into pages, with buttons at the top and bottom.

📡 Platforms

Your streaming services and game consoles live in one central list, each in its own section.

➕ New stuff

Use + Series, + Movie or + Game at the top right to add something new.

✅ Tracking what you watch

👆 One click, all caught up

Click an episode number and everything before it gets marked too. Mark season watched does the same for earlier seasons.

⏪ Oops, went too far?

Click an earlier watched episode to jump back to it. Everything after it gets unmarked, including the watch dates, so click carefully!

↩️ Just one step back

Click your last watched episode to unmark only that one. Clear season unmarks a single season.

⏭️ Next up

The Watched S01E02 button marks your next episode, right from the title page or home.

➕ New seasons

Click + New season on a series' page and say how many episodes it has. The pencil next to a season changes its size or lets you delete it, if it's the last one.

🔎 Season & episode pages

Click a season's name to see all its episodes, then click an episode for its own page. Each one has an Edit button for durations, release dates and descriptions.

🗓️ Upcoming & Just released

Home shows releases from the last 30 days and the next 90. New season coming? Give its first episode a release date. ✨

🎮 Tracking your games

Games work just like movies, but with a controller in your hands. Open a game and click Planned, Playing, Completed or Dropped. 🕹️

  • 👥 Tick Singleplayer, Multiplayer or both under Edit. Each one keeps its own status, shown as SP and MP in your lists.
  • ♾️ Multiplayer never ends, so it can't be marked Completed.
  • ⏱️ Keep count of your hours played under Edit. We won't judge.
  • 🏠 Games you're playing show up under Playing now on the home page.
  • 🏆 Mark one Completed and today becomes its finish date, unless you already set one.
  • 📦 Click Wanted or Owned on a game, or neither. The Games tab lists favorites first, then wanted, owned and the rest, and you can filter by any of them.

🗂️ Plans

Plans are playlists for real life! Mix series, movies and games any way you like: family movie night 👨‍👩‍👧, game night with friends 🎲, couch time with your S.O. 🛋️, or a solo weekend 🧘.

  • ➕ Make one on the Plans tab, then add titles from its page.
  • 👆 Or click a plan's name on any title's page to add or remove it in one go.
  • 🏷️ The little icon on each row tells you if it's a series, a movie or a game.
  • 📌 Planned is built in too, and fills itself with everything you've marked Planned.
  • 💖 Favorites is built in and always there. It holds everything with a filled heart, so click a heart to add or remove something.

👯 Duo

Wondering what to watch or play with someone? Ask them for the file from their Data tab, then add it on the Duo tab. You'll get lists of what you could enjoy together. 🍿

  • 🛡️ Duo only compares. Your library doesn't change.
  • 🌱 Dropped doesn't count here, so a title you drifted away from can still come up.
  • 📌 The file stays until you replace or forget it, so your lists are there whenever you come back.
  • 🔄 Their lists are as old as their file, so ask for a new one from time to time.
  • 🔎 Titles match by type, name and year. A missing year still matches, but two different years don't.

🚦 How status works

New titles start with no status at all. Planned is your call! Click a status on a title's page to set it, and click it again to clear it.

  • 📌 Planned puts it on your Up next list.
  • ▶️ Watching turns on by itself once you mark a series' first episode.
  • 🏁 Completed marks every episode of a series watched.
  • 🗑️ Dropped sticks until you pick something else. No hard feelings.

💡 Greyed-out buttons on a series don't match your episodes. Add a new season to a finished show and it hops back to Watching.

💖 Favorites, descriptions & notes

  • ⭐ Click the stars on a title or episode to rate it from 1 to 5. Click your current rating to clear it.
  • 🔗 Give series, movies or games the same franchise under Edit to link them. Series, movies and games stay in separate franchises, even with the same name. Each one then points to the titles released just before and after it.
  • 🩷 Click the heart next to a title's name to make it a favorite. Favorites go to the top of their tab.
  • 🎭 Pick genres and an age restriction under Edit, then click one on a title's page to see everything like it.
  • 🏢 Add a producer to any series, movie or game under Edit.
  • 📝 The description says what it's about, and notes are all yours. Both are under Edit.
  • 👀 Hover over an episode number for a quick peek at its details.

📡 Where to watch

Add your streaming platforms and game consoles on the Platforms tab, then tick them when you edit a title. Series and movies offer streaming platforms, and games offer consoles, which can have a release year. Each platform has a page listing everything you've got on it. 🎉

Got a long list? Put it under streaming_service: or game_console: in info/platform.yaml and run make import.

📥 Adding lots of titles at once

List your titles in info/movie.yaml, info/series.yaml and info/game.yaml, then run make import.

📄 Show me an example 
# platform.yaml
streaming_service:
  - name: Netflix
    url: https://www.netflix.com
    image: netflix.png
game_console:
  - name: PlayStation 5
    release_year: 2020

# movie.yaml
movie:
  - name: Arrival
    year: 2016
    status: completed
    rating: 5
    duration: 116
    genres: [Drama, Sci-Fi]
    age_restriction: 13+
    release_date: 2016-11-11
    platforms: [Netflix]
    description: A linguist is asked to talk to the aliens.
    notes: Worth a rewatch.
    image: arrival.jpg

# series.yaml
series:
  - name: The Wire
    year: 2002
    seasons: [13, 12, 12, 13, 10]
    watched_through: S02E04
    episodes:
      S01E01:
        duration: 60
        release_date: 2002-06-02
        description: The first episode.

# game.yaml
game:
  - name: Hades
    year: 2020
    producer: Supergiant Games
    status: playing
    multiplayer: true
    multiplayer_status: planned
    hours_played: 42
    ownership: owned
    platforms: [Steam]

👍 Importing again is safe. It won't undo anything you changed here, and only fills in what's still blank.

🖼️ Adding images

  1. Drop a JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or AVIF into the matching folder: info/images/movie/, series/, game/ or platform/.
  2. Add its file name to the title or platform, such as image: arrival.jpg.
  3. Run make import. Ta-da! ✨

💾 Taking your data with you

The Data tab saves your watch statuses, ratings, favorites and ownership to one small file, and loads such a file back in, on this computer or another one.

  • ⬇️ Download covers every title you've given a status, rating, watch progress, favorite or ownership. Notes, hours played and images stay out.
  • ⬆️ Load overwrites those titles with what the file says, and adds the ones you don't have yet. Anything that isn't in the file stays as it is.
  • 🛟 Plasma saves a copy of your database next to it before every load, just in case.

🚪 Leaving plasma

The door button at the top right stops plasma and closes the page. Your changes save as you go, and anything typed into an open form is saved first.

🖐️ Browsers only let pages close themselves sometimes. If the tab stays open, plasma has still stopped, so just close it yourself. Run make serve to come back!
