Help

Plasma keeps track of the movies and TV series you watch. Everything stays on your own computer.

Getting around

  • Home (click plasma) shows your stats, the series you're in the middle of, and what's up next.
  • Library lists everything, and you can search it or filter by status and favorites.
  • Movies and Series show just one kind.
  • + Movie and + Series add a new title.

Tracking what you watch

  • New titles have no status. Click a status on a title's page to set it: Planned, Watching, Completed or Dropped. Click the highlighted one again to clear it.
  • Click an episode number to mark it watched. Every episode before it is marked too.
  • Mark season watched also marks all earlier seasons.
  • Click an earlier watched episode to go back to it. Everything after it is unmarked, along with its watch dates.
  • Click your last watched episode to unmark just that one.
  • Clear season only unmarks that season.
  • The Watched S01E02 button marks your next episode, and it also appears on the home page.

How series status works

A series' status follows its episodes: some watched is Watching, and all of them is Completed. With nothing watched it has no status, unless you set it to Planned. The status buttons that don't match your episodes are disabled.

  • Completed marks every episode watched.
  • Dropped stays until you pick another status.
  • If you add a season to a completed series, it goes back to Watching.

Favorites and details

  • Click the heart next to a title's name to make it a favorite, and click again to undo it.
  • The description says what the title is about, and notes are your own thoughts. Change either with Edit.
  • Movies have a duration in minutes, also under Edit.
  • Episodes have a duration and description. Click Episode details on a season to edit them, and hover over an episode number to see them.

Adding titles from files

You can add many titles at once by listing them in info/library.yaml, then running make import.

movies:
  - name: Arrival
    year: 2016
    status: completed
    rating: 9
    duration: 116
    description: A linguist is asked to talk to the aliens.
    notes: Worth a rewatch.
    image: arrival.jpg

series:
  - name: The Wire
    year: 2002
    seasons: [13, 12, 12, 13, 10]
    watched_through: S02E04
    episodes:
      S01E01:
        duration: 60
        description: The first episode.

Importing again is safe, because it won't undo anything you changed here. It adds new titles and episodes, and fills in a rating, duration, description or notes that are still blank.

Adding images

Images come from info/ and can't be uploaded from the app:

  1. Put the file in info/images/. It can be a JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or AVIF.
  2. Add its file name to the title in info/library.yaml, such as image: arrival.jpg.
  3. Run make import.

Leaving Plasma

The door button in the top right corner stops Plasma and closes the page. Changes are saved as you make them, and anything you've typed into an open form is saved first.

Browsers only let a page close itself in some cases. If yours doesn't, Plasma is still stopped and you can close the tab yourself. Run make serve to start it again.
